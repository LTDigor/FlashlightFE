# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Independent rechargeable flashlight and headlamp. The headlamp crafts directly from raw materials; there is no empty headband, mounting or disassembly recipe.
- Optional Curios integration uses existing functional `head` slots without adding or resizing slots. Without a usable head slot, the headlamp equips in Minecraft's vanilla head slot.
- Original industrial item models, moving handheld button, and a forehead lamp with lens-only full-bright rendering.
- Configurable beam range, angle, brightness and edge softness; bounded circular light with optional LambDynamicLights and block-light fallback otherwise.
- Authoritative FE use and equipment synchronization, overlapping multiplayer beams, safe fallback for mixed clients, and cleanup across world changes.
- This is the authoritative initial version. Previous development items and worlds are not migrated.
